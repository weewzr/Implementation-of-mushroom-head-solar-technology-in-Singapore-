# Governing Equations

## Solar incidence
$$dP_{dir}=\eta(T)DNI(t)V(t)[\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf s(t)]_+dA.$$

## Isotropic diffuse first approximation
$$dP_{diff}=\eta(T)DHI(t)\frac{1+\cos\beta}{2}dA.$$

## Paraboloidal mushroom
$$z(r)=h\left(1-\frac{r^2}{R^2}\right),\qquad k=h/R.$$

$$\frac{A_{PV}}{A_{foot}}=\frac{(1+4k^2)^{3/2}-1}{6k^2}.$$

## Land multiplication factor
$$M_L=\frac{E_{3D}/A_{land}}{E_{flat}/A_{land}}=\Pi\eta_{pack}.$$

## Rotation
$$L=I\omega,\qquad E_k=\frac12I\omega^2,$$
$$\tau=I\ddot\theta+\tau_f+\tau_w+\tau_g.$$

## Wind
$$F_D=\frac12\rho C_DA_{proj}v^2,\qquad \tau_w\simeq F_Dr_{CP}.$$

## Temperature correction
$$\eta(T_c)=\eta_{ref}[1+\gamma(T_c-T_{ref})].$$
